Add a profile

Create a private draft in the guided editor, preview it, and submit it for moderator review when you are ready.

Before you start

  • Share only what you have permission to share, including any photos.
  • Writing about someone else? You need appropriate authorisation from that person, or from whoever is entitled to act for them.
  • Do not include birth dates, addresses, private contact details, passwords or account access. Approved profiles become public.
  • Removal is possible, but copies, forks and Git history may persist elsewhere afterwards.

Steps

  1. Create an account with a private email and strong password.
  2. Use the dashboard editor to save and preview a private draft.
  3. Confirm your publication consent and submit the draft for review.
  4. Read private rejection feedback in your dashboard, if changes are needed.
  5. Unpublish or delete your profile from the dashboard at any time.

What happens next

Server validation confirms the draft is valid. A moderator then reviews it against the moderation checklist — consent, private information, impersonation, harassment and unauthorised copyrighted material — and either approves it or explains what needs changing. Moderators are volunteers, so review may take a while.
