Stories people chose to leave behind

Legacy is a community archive. People write about who they are, what they value, and what they made — in their own words — and submit it for review.

What this project is

A profile here is a short, human page: an introduction, a story, the things you believe, a few things you made, and what you hope other people carry forward. Owners save private drafts and explicitly submit them for moderator approval before publication.

What this project is not

This project aims to help people preserve and share memories. It cannot promise permanent hosting, and it cannot promise that anyone will never be forgotten. Repositories, websites and organisations all end. Keep your own independent backups of anything that matters to you — see backups and stewardship.

Legacy is not a credential vault, not a place for passwords or account access, and not an official memorial verification service. Nothing here is verified as belonging to a particular real person.

Consent comes first

Only share what you have permission to share. Submissions about someone else need appropriate authorisation from that person or from whoever is entitled to act for them. Because this repository is public, copies, forks and Git history may continue to exist after content is removed here. Read the privacy, consent and removal guidance before you contribute.
