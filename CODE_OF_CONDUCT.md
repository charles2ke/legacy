# Code of Conduct

This project exists to hold people's stories. That only works if everyone here is
treated with care.

## Our commitment

We want participation in Legacy to be a respectful experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Write and review with kindness, especially when a submission is about grief,
  illness, or someone who has died.
- Assume good faith, ask before assuming, and accept that people may decline to
  explain personal circumstances.
- Respect other people's privacy and their decisions about what to share.
- Accept maintainer decisions about what is appropriate for a public archive.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information — addresses, contact details,
  identity documents, medical details — without their explicit permission.
- Impersonating another person, living or dead, or submitting a profile about
  someone without appropriate authorisation.
- Submitting text or media you do not have the right to share.
- Using this repository to store or exchange credentials or account access.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits
and profile content — and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, you can open an issue describing what
happened. **Issues are public: do not include private details, identity documents,
medical information or contact details.** If the matter cannot be described in
public, say only that in the issue and ask a maintainer for a private contact
route.

> **Maintainer configuration needed.** This repository does not currently publish a
> private reporting channel, and no such address is claimed to exist. A maintainer
> who wants one should add a real, monitored contact here and in
> [docs/PRIVACY.md](docs/PRIVACY.md), and consider enabling GitHub's
> [private vulnerability and security advisory features](https://docs.github.com/code-security)
> and restricted discussions. Until then, reports arrive only through public issues
> and pull requests.

Conduct reports are different from security vulnerabilities. For a flaw in the
site or workflows, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, decline or remove contributions, comments and profiles
that break this code of conduct, and may block accounts that continue to do so.
Enforcement is carried out by volunteers and is not guaranteed to be immediate.
